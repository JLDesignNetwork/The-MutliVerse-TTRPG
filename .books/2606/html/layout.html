SURVIVE THE COLLISION

Reality has detonated. The laws of physics, magic, and cyberspace collide inside volatile dimensional rifts. As a Wayfarer, you walk the razor's edge between totalitarian temporal order and total cosmic collapse.

The Multiverse TTRPG Framework delivers a unified, math-less d10 system engineered for limitless genre crossing:

  • No Math Arithmetic: Count 7+ successes, strip dice for difficulty.
  • Universal Tag-Permissions: Seamlessly translate characters from any system.
  • Dynamic Power Scales: Bounded vertical scaling from street-level to cosmic godheads.
  • Dual-Track Harm: 9-Stage Trauma and Stability tracks.
