# Gemini Image Prompts for Homebrewery Book

Use these prompts in Gemini Web to generate full-page splashes and spot art for your Homebrewery layout. 
When Gemini generates an image, right-click, copy the image address, and paste it into the markdown like this:
`![Front Cover](YOUR_IMAGE_LINK) {position:absolute, top:0px, left:0px, width:100%}`

## 1. Front Cover
> **Prompt:** "Portrait orientation, 9:16 aspect ratio. A hyper-detailed, cinematic digital painting of a massive, reality-tearing dimensional rift suspended over a sprawling, neon-lit cyberpunk megacity. On the other side of the glowing purple rift, a medieval high-fantasy castle and dragons can be seen. Dark, gritty atmosphere, epic scale, concept art."

## 2. Book I: The Shattered Cosmos (Splash Page)
> **Prompt:** "Portrait orientation, 9:16 aspect ratio. A panoramic concept art view of Zero-Point Station, an impossibly massive, gothic-industrial space station floating in a void of swirling nebulae and shattered planet fragments. Countless ships from different time periods and sci-fi universes are docked. Grimdark aesthetic."

## 3. Book II: The Laws of Reality (Splash Page)
> **Prompt:** "Portrait orientation, 9:16 aspect ratio. A glowing, holographic projection of a ten-sided die (d10) floating above a high-tech tactical table. The geometric crystal is inscribed with glowing magical runes and circuitry. Shadowy figures of different species (a cyborg, an elf, a space marine) stand around the table. Cinematic lighting."

## 4. Book III: The Wayfarer's Path (Splash Page)
> **Prompt:** "Portrait orientation, 9:16 aspect ratio. A close-up of a heavily armored Wayfarer standing at a crossroads in a post-apocalyptic wasteland. They are holding a glowing magical staff in one hand and a futuristic plasma rifle in the other. Their armor is a patchwork of medieval plate mail and cyberpunk cybernetics."

## 5. Book IV: Chronicles of the Shattered (Splash Page)
> **Prompt:** "Portrait orientation, 9:16 aspect ratio. An impossibly large, ancient library where the bookshelves stretch into infinity. The books are glowing with different colors. In the center, a hooded figure is reading a holographic scroll that projects shifting images of different universes. Dark academia meets sci-fi."

## 6. Spot Art: The Rift-Guard
> **Prompt:** "Square orientation, 1:1 aspect ratio. A crest or emblem design for a trans-dimensional police force called 'The Rift-Guard'. The emblem features a shield bisected by a jagged lightning bolt, with a sci-fi gear on one side and a fantasy sword on the other. Metallic texture, worn and battle-scarred, isolated on a white background."
> **Image Tag:** `![Rift-Guard Crest](YOUR_IMAGE_LINK) {width:300px, mix-blend-mode:multiply}` *(Place this anywhere in the Factions section)*

## 7. Back Cover
> **Prompt:** "Portrait orientation, 9:16 aspect ratio. The back cover of a weathered, ancient tome. A glowing keyhole sits in the center of the leather binding, emitting a faint cosmic light. Sci-fi circuitry subtly weaves through the leather. Minimalist, dark, atmospheric."
> **Image Tag:** `![Back Cover](YOUR_IMAGE_LINK) {position:absolute, top:0px, left:0px, width:100%}`
