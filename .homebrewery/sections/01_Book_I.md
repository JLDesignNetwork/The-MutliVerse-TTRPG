# 01 - The Shattering

> "Do not look at the rifts as doorways. Look at them as wounds. The multiverse is bleeding out, and the Architects are trying to cauterize the veins with us trapped inside. Keep your head down, keep your slots charged, and whatever you do—don't let them catch you tampering with the timeline."
> — Intercepted Transmission, Wayfarer Designation: 'Vagabond'

The Multiverse was not always a fractured mirror. It was once a singular, unified reality. 

Eons ago, an ancient civilization—operating at the absolute pinnacle of existence, a theoretical [Power Scale: 4]—attempted to unify time itself. They sought to bind every probability into a single, perfect timeline. Instead, their Hubris tore reality apart. 

This cataclysm is known as **The Shattering**. The singular universe violently detonated into infinite, colliding dimensions. The laws of physics, magic, and digital space were ripped from their foundational moorings and scrambled across realities.

Every piece of rift-tech, every dimensional drive, and every anomalous artifact is actually *archaeo-tech*—dangerous, reverse-engineered fragments from this fallen civilization. You and your fellow Wayfarers are not gods traversing the planes; you are scavengers playing with matches in a powder keg. Every jump between realities is a violent trespassing across a cosmic wound.

# 02 - Zero-Point Station

> "The smell hits you first. Ozone, blood, and burnt circuitry. Then the noise—a million dialects of survival echoing off non-Euclidean walls. They call it a station. I call it a life raft."
> — Wayfarer's Journal, Entry 41

In the wake of The Shattering, reality fractured. But at the dead center of the explosion, a pocket of null-space remained. Here lies **Zero-Point Station**, commonly known simply as **The Hub**.

The Hub is a massive, sprawling, non-Euclidean construct residing entirely outside of individual dimensions. It is the ultimate neutral zone where the boundaries of the Multiverse violently, yet peacefully, coexist. Here, the [Material], [Digital], and [Metaphysical] domains overlap seamlessly.

It is a desperate, bustling trade port and the last refuge of the displaced. In the neon-lit bazaars of the lower wards, a cyberpunk hacker from a dystopian metropolis might barter stolen subroutines for a magical grimoire peddled by a medieval wizard. It is a place of infinite possibility and infinite danger, where the only universal currency is survival.

# 03 - Factions and The Rift-Guard

> "They don't care if a million innocent souls are consumed by the Void. They only care that the math balances. If pruning your reality keeps the rest of the sector stable, you won't even see the firing squad that pulls the trigger."
> — Wayfarer's Journal, Entry 12

Existence is governed by a central, terrifying authority: **The Architects**. 

A consortium of [Power Scale: 4] entities, The Architects survived The Shattering and now view the Multiverse as a diseased organism in need of aggressive triage. They do not rule; they manage. They enforce the **Stability Protocols**, strict laws designed to prevent further dimensional collapse. 

To The Architects, containment is the only priority. Morality is a mathematical error. If a dimension becomes too anomalous or threatens the integrity of the Hub, they will willingly "prune" it—erasing the entire timeline to protect the remaining Multiverse.

### The Rift-Guard
The Architects' enforcers are the **Rift-Guard**. Drawn from a thousand realities, they are the dimensional police force. Heavy-handed and dogmatic, they patrol the Hub and priority dimensions, hunting down anomalies, enforcing quarantines, and neutralizing threats to dimensional stability. 

### Allegiance and The Wayfarers
You are a Wayfarer, displaced by the chaotic currents of the Multiverse. Your allegiance is more than a tag on your sheet; it is a philosophical stance on how to survive the end of everything.

* **The Authority (The Rift-Guard):** Do you side with the Architects? Will you enforce order, even if it means destroying free will and sacrificing entire worlds for the "greater good"?
* **The Fugitives (Syndicates & Free-Runners):** Do you rebel against the pruning? Do you scavenge archaeo-tech to protect your own slice of reality, constantly dodging the Rift-Guard and surviving in the shadows?
* **The Unaligned:** Do you walk the razor's edge, selling your skills to the highest bidder in the bustling markets of Zero-Point Station?

# 04 - The Grand Purpose

> "We aren't heroes. We are scavengers with a very specific set of skills, trying to make sense of a jigsaw puzzle where half the pieces are on fire and the other half are actively trying to kill us."
> — Intercepted Transmission, Wayfarer Designation: 'Vagabond'

The Multiverse TTRPG is not a game about saving the world. It is a game about surviving the collision of infinite worlds. 

### The Engine of Play
As Wayfarers, you are driven by mystery, curiosity, and the raw instinct to survive. You are navigating a multiverse where the specific dilemmas, horrors, and politics of infinite realities are violently bleeding into one another. 

The Grand Purpose of your journey is defined by three pillars:
1. **Scavenge the Ruins:** You are constantly hunting for archaeo-tech, resources, and lore. Every piece of powerful technology or magic is a fragment of the civilization that broke the world.
2. **Navigate the Collision:** You must negotiate, fight, and think your way out of situations where high-fantasy magic collides with cyberpunk corporate espionage. The rules of reality are constantly in flux, and only the adaptable survive.
3. **Evade the Threat:** Whether you are dodging the dogmatic enforcement of the Rift-Guard, fleeing a cosmic anomaly, or surviving a dimensional collapse, the Multiverse is actively pushing back against your existence. 

Your story is one of desperate alliances, tactical scavenging, and the sheer force of will required to carve out a life in the shadows of the Shattered Cosmos.
