# 04 - The Threat System

> "You think you can just bend reality until it breaks and nobody will notice? The Hub notices everything. And it always corrects the ledger."
> — Intercepted Transmission, Wayfarer Designation: 'Cipher'

The Multiverse is a fragile construct, heavily monitored by The Architects and inherently hostile to destabilization. The **Threat Track** represents this escalating pressure. It merges narrative consequences with mechanical danger, tracking your crew's overall "Warrant" and the likelihood of dimensional anomalies.

## The Threat Track

The Threat Track is a shared pool managed by the Game Master, ranging from 1 to 10. It represents the immediate sector's instability and the local Architect security response.

### Escalating the Track
The track advances when players push the limits of reality or draw undue attention. The GM advances the track by +1 when:
*   A player uses a [System: Volatile] tag and fails the roll.
*   A player exhausts their Slot capacity and "Pushes the Limit."
*   A massive explosion or heavily destructive weapon is utilized in a populated or monitored zone.
*   The crew deliberately overrides a zero-point gate or corrupts a local timeline.
*   The crew ignores a direct Architect mandate.

## Threat Thresholds & Consequences

As the track advances, the consequences become increasingly dire. The GM may trigger a consequence appropriate for the current tier.

### Tier 1: Systemic Friction (Track 1-3)
*The sector is taking notice. Reality is stressed.*
*   **Communication Interference:** Long-range comms drop out or become flooded with static and Architect telemetry data.
*   **Minor Glitches:** Synthetics experience harmless but disturbing sensory hallucinations. Magic users feel an uncomfortable "pull" on their aura.
*   **Security Sweeps:** Local automated drones or low-level corporate security enter the area to investigate anomalies.

### Tier 2: The Pushback (Track 4-6)
*The environment becomes hostile. Automated corrections begin.*
*   **The Anomaly:** A localized dimensional hazard forms. Gravity reverses in a single room, a localized time-loop traps a character for 1 round, or shadows become solid, difficult terrain.
*   **Warrant Escalation:** The crew is flagged as "Persons of Interest." Bounties are posted to the local undercity network.
*   **Rift-Guard Deployment:** A small, specialized squad of Rift-Guard executes a tactical drop into the sector.

### Tier 3: Critical Instability (Track 7-9)
*Reality is actively attempting to crush the anomaly (you).*
*   **Severe Anomalies:** A hostile rift opens, pulling in environmental hazards or depositing predatory entities from an unknown dimension.
*   **The Purge Order:** The crew's Warrant is escalated to "Execute on Sight." Any nearby faction with ties to the Architects will immediately attack.
*   **Architect Overrides:** Doors lock, life support drops, or the very geometry of the facility begins shifting to trap the crew.

### Tier 4: The Erasure Event (Track 10)
*The Architects intervene directly.*
*   An **Architect Prime** manifests, or a **Zero-Point Erasure** weapon is deployed. This is a survival scenario; there is no fighting a Tier 4 response. The crew has exactly 3 rounds to escape the sector before the area and everything in it is completely reset or deleted from the Hub.

## Cooling the Track
The Threat Track does not reset easily.
*   **Fleeing the Sector:** Moving to a completely different, disconnected region of the Hub reduces the track by half (rounded down).
*   **Laying Low:** Taking a Long Rest in a completely secure, unregistered safehouse permanently reduces the Threat Track by a flat 2 stages.
*   **Direct Bribes / Data Wipes:** Spending significant resources (credits, rare tech, or calling in extreme favors) to wipe the crew's signatures from the local grid.
