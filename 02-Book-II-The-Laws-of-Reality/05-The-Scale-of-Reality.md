# Power Scales (Integration Scales)

Power Scales abstract vertical power scaling and technological eras. This is what handles multi-genre compatibility, defining what happens when a knight attacks a tank.

## The Four Power Scales

* **[System: Power Scale 1 Primal & Archaic]:** Low-tech, medieval fantasy, or mundane human baselines. Includes flintlocks, traditional swords, and standard beasts.
* **[System: Power Scale 2 Augmented & Industrial]:** Modern firearms, steampunk mechanisms, early cybernetics, and intermediate supernatural potencies.
* **[System: Power Scale 3 Advanced & Stellar]:** High sci-fi, starships, powered mechs, heavy power armor, elder supernatural entities, and cataclysmic magical arts. Includes classic Mega-Damage Capacity (M.D.C.) profiles.
* **[System: Power Scale 4 Cosmic & Singular]:** Reality-warping deities, outer dimensional godheads, and absolute planetary threats.

## Scale Dominance & Friction

When cross-scale conflicts occur, **Scale Dominance** applies. 

Lower-scale entities suffering checks against or attempting to bypass the defenses of higher-scale entities encounter severe **Friction**. 
* This results in automatic failure or heavily enforced disadvantage.
* The only way to bypass Friction is if the lower-scale entity possesses matching protective tags, exploits specific vulnerabilities, or utilizes Scale Shifting.

### The Scale Hard-Cap
To prevent astronomical luck from breaking Power Scale logic:
* An attack cannot inflict damage on an entity that is 2 or more Power Scales higher unless the attacker possesses a specific [Gear: Piercing] or [Status: Flatline] tag that matches the target's specific composition tag (e.g., [Path Feature: Anti-Alloy] weaponry). Without the correct tag, dice outcomes of 10 do not explode against superior Scales; they are treated as standard successes.

### [System: Scale Shift +/- X]
Activating a [System: Scale Shift] is a **Mastery-level action** that temporarily alters an entity's power scale, allowing them to punch above their weight class momentarily to avoid Friction.
* **Cost:** It requires the player to possess a [Path Feature: Mastery Tag] (e.g., [Path Feature: Combat Stimulant Expert], [Path Feature: Tactical Genius]). 
* **Activation:** Burning 1 Slot and 2 AP. 
* **Effect:** It provides a [System: Scale Shift 1] for the duration of one encounter, allowing the player to act as if they are one Power Scale higher for the purpose of avoiding Friction.
