*   **[Class Feature: Wild Shape (CR 1/4 or below, no flying or swim speed)]:** You can magically assume the shape of a beast you have seen before with a Challenge Rating of 1/4 or lower, and no flying or swimming speed. This transformation costs 2 AP and lasts for {{MASTERY_TIER}} hours, or until you revert. You can revert to your normal form early by spending 1 AP as a 1 AP. You automatically revert if you suffer [Status: Clinical Death], reach the beast form's Trauma Threshold, or die. When you transform, your [Approach: Force], [Approach: Precision], and [Approach: Fortitude] Approaches, along with your Trauma Threshold, are replaced by those of the beast. You retain your [Approach: Insight] and [Approach: Presence] Approaches, as well as your alignment and personality. You retain all your skill and saving throw proficiencies, gaining those of the beast as well; if the beast's proficiency bonus for a given skill or save is higher, use its bonus. If you revert due to reaching the beast form's Trauma Threshold, any excess Trauma carries over to your normal form. You cannot cast spells, and your ability to speak or take actions requiring hands is limited by your beast form. You retain the benefit of your class, race, or other features if physically capable in the new form, but not special senses unless the beast form also possesses them. You choose whether your equipment falls, merges, or is worn; worn equipment functions normally if practical for the new form.
