# The Multiverse – Origins, Authority, and Conflict

## Origins: The Shattering
The Multiverse was a singular reality torn apart by an ancient [System: Power Scale 4] civilization attempting to unify time. This cataclysm created the infinite dimensions. All current rift technology is "archaeo-tech," reverse-engineered from these original fragments.

## The Hub: Zero-Point Station
The only non-Euclidean construct outside of individual dimensions where [Domain: Material], [Domain: Digital], and [Domain: Metaphysical] domains coexist. It serves as the primary neutral trade hub.

## Governance: The Architects & The Rift-Guard
The "Architects" (a consortium of [System: Power Scale 4] entities) enforce "Stability Protocols" via the Rift-Guard. They prioritize containment over morality and will prune entire dimensions to protect the integrity of the Multiverse.

## The Authority & Fugitive Factions
Players define their allegiance through Faction Tags, which act as Mastery-level progression choices.

### Authority (Stability-Focused)
*   [Lore: Rift-Guard]: Stabilize [System: Volatile] actions with 1 AP (no Slot). Gains [System: Advantage 2] to track anomalies.
*   [Path Feature: Entropy-Agent]: Force a target to re-roll a successful action once per session.
*   [Path Feature: Seal-Breaker]: Force-close any active Rift within 300 feet.

### Fugitive (Outlaw-Focused)
*   [Path Feature: Wayfarer]: Open a "Dirty Rift" for 2 AP (grants the [System: Volatile] tag to the action).
*   [Path Feature: Void-Walker]: Invisible to Architect tracking tech; never triggers alarms.
*   [System: Anomaly]: Immune to [System: Erasure]; ignore Scale Friction against [System: Power Scale 4] entities.

## The [System: Warrant] Track
The [System: Warrant] Track is a 3-Stage escalating threat tracker based on reckless dimensional behavior.

*   **Trigger:** Using [System: Volatile] abilities or breaking the Law of Conservation (introducing high-power-scale tech into low-power-scale worlds).
*   **Stage 1 - Observed:** Draws local attention or minor investigation.
*   **Stage 2 - Hunted:** Draws Rift-Guard sentinels to actively pursue the anomaly.
*   **Stage 3 - Erased:** Draws Architect-level intervention, resulting in devastating Boss encounters geared toward complete [System: Erasure].
