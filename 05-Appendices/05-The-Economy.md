# The Economy

Currency is abstracted to facilitate fast narrative play and cross-dimensional parity without tracking specific gold pieces or credits.

## The Multiverse Exchange Matrix

Wealth is represented as a persistent Grade that dictates what a character can effortlessly acquire.

* **[System: Wealth 0] Destitute:** Can barely afford standard daily rations. Survival is day-to-day.
* **[System: Wealth 1] Working Class:** Can afford basic ammunition, cheap inns, minor gear repairs, and standard travel fare.
* **[System: Wealth 2] Professional:** Capable of maintaining a reliable vehicle, paying for sterile surgical procedures, and affording standard bribes.
* **[System: Wealth 3] Elite:** Has access to heavy or illegal ordnance, specialized cybernetics, or ownership of a small starship.
* **[System: Wealth 4] Aristocrat / Executive:** Can field a private mercenary army, purchase top-tier resurrection magic, or own vast corporate assets.
* **[System: Wealth 5] Cosmic:** Capable of crashing planetary economies or purchasing entire orbital stations.

## Dimensional Transit & Currency Translation

The stability of the portal or rift dictates the value of physical currency across universes.

* **Stable Rifts:** Officially recognized or inherently stable rifts automatically convert currency values, translating the host's wealth grade directly into the local equivalent (e.g., gold bars naturally swap out for high-value credit chips).
* **Anomalous Rifts:** Unstable or violent rifts do not convert assets. The character's physical wealth is branded with the [Status: Incompatible] tag, dropping their functional purchasing power to [System: Wealth 0] until they find a specialized broker or convert raw materials.
