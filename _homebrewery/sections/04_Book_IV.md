# The Infinite Archive

The Multiverse TTRPG is designed to seamlessly integrate characters, enemies, and concepts from entirely different tabletop roleplaying games. The Infinite Archive is a conversion bridge that maps classes, roles, and archetypes from other systems into the Tag-Permission framework.

## The Universal Asset Reference Table

When importing an adversary or allowing a player to port a character from another system, use this table as a baseline translation guide. 

*   **System of Origin:** The native TTRPG system.
*   **Asset Name:** The class, archetype, or monster name.
*   **Recommended Origin Tag:** The baseline physiological state.
*   **Class Tag (Archetype):** Wayfarers may choose one of the listed Class Tags for their Archetype to define their core mechanical permission, granting greater flexibility to their origin. This tag is permanent.
*   **Recommended Path Tags (Choose 2):** Suggested Path Tags to fulfill the 1+2 Structure.
*   **Power Scale Baseline:** The default Power Scale (1-4) they operate within.
*   **Default Domain:** The primary type of interaction or damage they output.

## Cross-Contamination (The Rift-Beast Mechanic)

When entities (especially monsters and fauna) enter a new dimension through a volatile rift, they do not simply arrive intact. The physics of dimensional travel warp their physiology, creating "Cross-Contamination."

### The Mechanic
When a creature enters the battlefield via an unstable or [Volatile] rift, they immediately gain the [Anomaly] tag. This tag represents a fusion of physical mutation and localized environmental hazard.

The GM rolls **1d10** on the Anomaly Table below to determine the specific Cross-Contamination effect applied to the creature:

| 1d10 | Contamination Effect | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Domain Shift (Mutation)** | The creature's physiological attacks permanently shift to a new domain (e.g., an [Organic] wolf's bite now deals [Digital] data-corruption trauma). |
| **2** | **Volatile Aura (Hazard)** | The creature emanates a 30ft aura of dimensional static. Any entity starting its turn inside the aura suffers [Disadvantage: 1] to all technological or magical checks. |
| **3** | **Ablative Carapace (Mutation)** | Dimensional friction calcifies onto the creature upon entry. It gains an [Trait: Ablative 2] armor tag, regardless of its native stats. |
| **4** | **Temporal Stutter (Hazard)** | The creature phases in and out of the current timeline. It gains the Passive Defense of a standard evasion automatically without spending AP, requiring attackers to generate at least 2 successes to hit it. |
| **5** | **Energy Feedback (Hazard)** | The creature's form is supercharged. Striking the creature with any melee attack triggers a localized blast. The attacker immediately takes 1 Trauma Point unless they pass a Force or Endurance check. |
| **6** | **Eldritch Awakening (Mutation)** | The creature absorbs the void. It jumps +1 Power Scale and gains the [Ascendant] origin. However, its form rapidly degrades; it dies instantly at the end of the combat encounter. |

# Running Adversaries

Game Masters utilize four classifications of adversary complexity to balance the action economy and mental bandwidth.

## The Adversary Hierarchy

1. **Minions (The Swarm):**
   * **Threat Rating:** 2 Dice.
   * **Health:** Binary (1 Major Wound kills them).
   * **Economy:** 1 AP per turn.

2. **Veterans (The Tactical Baseline):**
   * **Threat Rating:** 3 Dice.
   * **Health:** Requires 2 hits (1 to strip an [Ablative] armor tag, and 1 to kill).
   * **Economy:** 2 AP per turn.

3. **Elites (The Tactical Threat):**
   * **Threat Rating:** Targeted specific stat approaches.
   * **Health:** Requires 2 full Major Wounds to kill.
   * **Economy:** 3 AP per turn.
   * **Special:** Always possesses at least one signature Tag.

4. **Bosses (The Mirror Match):**
   * **Threat Rating:** Full Character Sheet with 5 Approaches.
   * **Health:** Utilizes the full 9-Stage Trauma Track.
   * **Economy:** 3 to 5 AP per turn depending on Scale Dominance.

> **Design Note:** When pitting players against enemies 2+ Power Scales higher, the GM must ensure the encounter includes secondary objectives or environmental vulnerabilities. Lower-scale players cannot bypass Scale Friction without these tactical elements, even with perfect dice rolls.

## Morale, Leadership, & The Breaking Point

Entities do not fight to the death unconditionally. Morale dictates combat flow and enemy retreats.

### The Baseline Morale Grades
* **[Morale: 1]:** Self-Preservation. Will break at the first sign of losing.
* **[Morale: 2]:** Professional. Will execute orders and hold lines until strategically unviable.
* **[Morale: 3]:** Zealot. Will fight through grievous injury for their cause.
* **[Mindless]:** Immune to morale breaking.

### The Motivational Anchors
Tags such as [Drive: Fear], [Drive: Loyalty], or [Drive: Dedication] dictate precisely how enemy units behave when their morale breaks.

### The Anatomy of a Break
When Morale is shattered, the enemies will default to one of these states based on their Anchors:
* **The Panicked Rout:** Drop weapons and sprint to Extreme Range.
* **The Desperate Rescue:** Gain the [Suppressed] tag to drag [Status: Clinical Death] allies out of the line of fire.
* **The Tactical Fallback:** Dedicate 2 AP to moving away safely, while utilizing 1 AP for suppressive covering fire.

### Origin & Innate Tag Permissions
*   **[Biotic]:** You are a living, biological organism. You require food, water, and oxygen, and are susceptible to organic toxins and diseases.
*   **[Darkvision]:** You ignore [System: Disadvantage 1] penalties for acting in complete or near-total darkness.
*   **[Elemental Breath]:** You possess a biological weapon (e.g., fire, acid) that you can use at short range without a weapon.
*   **[Trait: Resistant X]:** You automatically ignore the first stage of trauma applied from your specific element or hazard type.
*   **[Attuned]:** You can passively sense the presence of active magic, ethereal entities, or rifts within your immediate vicinity.
*   **[Lucky]:** Once per session, you may force the GM to reroll a single die or anomaly result that directly affects you.
*   **[Relentless]:** The first time you would be [Status: Clinical Death] or drop to Trauma Stage 9, you instead remain conscious at Stage 8.
*   **[Intimidating]:** You gain [System: Advantage 1] on Resonance checks made to frighten, coerce, or bully an NPC.
*   **[Versatile]:** During character creation, you may select one additional Path Tag or one minor narrative permission.
*   **[Shadowed]:** You gain [Advantage: 1] on Vector rolls when attempting stealth in low light or dense cover.
*   **[Influential]:** Once per session, you may substitute your Resonance Approach for any other Approach roll by leveraging social capital.

### Class Tag Permissions
When you select your archetype's Class Tag, it grants a permanent narrative and mechanical permission.

**Barbarian**
*   **[Rage]:** You can enter a state of fury, granting [Trait: Resistant Kinetic] and [System: Advantage 1] on all melee Force checks, but preventing complex logical actions.
*   **[Juggernaut]:** You ignore movement penalties from heavy armor or carrying massive loads, and can smash through structural barriers without rolling.
*   **[Blood-Trance]:** You ignore all Status Effects from the Trauma Track until combat ends or you drop to Stage 9.

**Bard**
*   **[Inspire]:** You can spend an action to grant [System: Advantage 1] to an ally's next roll.
*   **[Lore-Keeper]:** You automatically know a relevant rumor or historical fact about any named location, faction, or artifact.
*   **[Skald]:** You can use Resonance instead of Force to intimidate or rally troops in mass combat.

**Cleric**
*   **[Divine Channel]:** You can spend Slots to perform miracles aligned with your deity's domain (e.g., healing, turning undead).
*   **[War Priest]:** You can wield heavy weapons and heavy armor without the [System: Disadvantage 1] penalty usually applied to magic users.
*   **[Life-Weaver]:** When using Triage to stabilize an ally, you restore two Trauma Stages instead of one.

**Druid**
*   **[Shapeshift]:** You can physically transform into beasts. While shifted, you use the physical tags of the beast instead of your own.
*   **[Nature's Wrath]:** You gain [System: Advantage 1] when manipulating environmental hazards (vines, weather, earth) to attack or trap foes.
*   **[Spirit-Walker]:** You can see and communicate with spirits, elementals, and the lingering echoes of the dead.

**Fighter**
*   **[Mastery: Weapon]:** Choose one weapon type. You always generate +1 Success on attacks with that weapon.
*   **[Tactician]:** You can assess a battlefield to identify the primary weakness or objective of the enemy forces automatically.
*   **[Champion]:** Your physical critical threshold is expanded; your Desperation Die explodes on a 9 or 10.

**Monk**
*   **[Ki Strike]:** Your unarmed strikes count as [Piercing] against standard kinetic armor.
*   **[Shadow-Step]:** You can instantly traverse short distances between areas of darkness without triggering movement penalties or overwatch.
*   **[Iron Body]:** You naturally possess [Trait: Ablative 1] armor even when unarmored.

**Paladin**
*   **[Divine Smite]:** You can spend 1 Slot after a successful melee hit to add a massive burst of [Radiant] trauma.
*   **[Aura of Protection]:** Allies standing adjacent to you gain your highest Resistance tag.
*   **[Oathbound]:** You gain [System: Advantage 1] on any check that directly furthers your sworn oath, but suffer [System: Disadvantage 1] if you act against it.

**Ranger**
*   **[Favored Terrain]:** You cannot be tracked, and you ignore all natural difficult terrain penalties.
*   **[Beast-Master]:** You command a loyal animal companion that acts independently on your turn without costing your AP.
*   **[Hunter's Mark]:** You can mark a target; you never lose track of them as long as they remain on the same plane of existence.

**Rogue**
*   **[Sneak Attack]:** If you attack a target who is distracted or unaware, your attack is automatically treated as if you rolled 1 additional Success.
*   **[Assassin]:** You gain [System: Advantage 1] on checks made to craft poisons, set traps, or strike from complete stealth.
*   **[Phantom]:** You leave no physical footprints, thermal signature, or scent trail unless you choose to.

**Sorcerer**
*   **[Metamagic]:** You can alter the tags of your spells on the fly (e.g., changing a [Thermal] blast to [Sonic], or changing [Loud] to [Silent]).
*   **[Wild Magic]:** You generate an extra d10 for spellcasting, but any non-success result triggers an immediate roll on the Anomaly Table.
*   **[Draconic Bloodline]:** You naturally possess [Power Scale: 2] physical durability and a passive breath weapon.

**Warlock**
*   **[Eldritch Pact]:** You can petition your patron for a supernatural favor or knowledge once per session, though it always comes with a narrative cost.
*   **[Hexblade]:** You can summon a bound weapon from the void that uses Cognition instead of Force for combat checks.
*   **[Mind-Thief]:** You can read the surface thoughts of any sentient creature you make physical contact with.

**Wizard**
*   **[Spellbook]:** You can permanently learn any spell or ritual you encounter by spending downtime to transcribe it.
*   **[Abjurer]:** You can project a protective barrier over yourself or an ally that absorbs 1 Trauma Stage per encounter.
*   **[Evoker]:** Your area-of-effect spells automatically exclude your allies from the blast radius.

### Class Tag Permissions
When you select your archetype's Class Tag, it grants a permanent narrative and mechanical permission.

**Space Marine Tactical**
*   **[Trait: Ablative 3]:** Your power armor naturally absorbs the first three stages of trauma in any combat encounter without degrading your health.
*   **[Astartes Physiology]:** You are immune to standard poisons, diseases, and extreme environments, and can survive in the vacuum of space for short periods.
*   **[Bolter Discipline]:** You ignore the [Recoil] penalty when firing heavy automatic weapons.

**Tech-Priest**
*   **[Machine Spirit]:** You can interface directly with digital systems, allowing you to "speak" to computers and machines to glean their intent or malfunctions.
*   **[Omnissiah's Blessing]:** You possess innate [Machine Empathy], granting [System: Advantage 1] on all mechanical repairs and enabling seamless use of Architect tech.

*   **[Authority]:** You wield the absolute authority of the Inquisition. Most Imperial citizens and local forces will obey your direct commands without question.
*   **[Ordo Specialist]:** Choose Xenos, Malleus (Demons), or Hereticus. You gain [System: Advantage 1] on all checks related to tracking, understanding, or fighting that specific enemy type.

**Commissar**
*   **[Execute]:** You can execute a fleeing or panicked ally to immediately restore all Morale or mental-stability tags to the rest of the squad.
*   **[Inspiring Terror]:** You can use Resonance to force enemies with lower Power Scales to surrender or break formation.
*   **[Iron Will]:** You are entirely immune to magical or psychic fear and mind-control effects.

**Imperial Guardsman**
*   **[Squad Tactics]:** Whenever you are adjacent to an ally, both of you gain [System: Advantage 1] on defensive maneuvers.
*   **[Hold the Line]:** You cannot be knocked prone or forcibly moved by attacks of the same or lower Power Scale.
*   **[Meatgrinder Survivor]:** You automatically succeed on Endurance checks made to survive extreme starvation, trench rot, or exhaustion.

**Sister of Battle**

*   **[Holy Promethium]:** Your thermal weapons (flamers, meltas) ignore [Trait: Resistant Thermal] tags on heretics, mutants, or demons.

**Sanctioned Psyker**

*   **[Warp Conduit]:** You can burn 1 stage of your own Trauma Track to cast a spell without spending an AP or a Slot.
*   **[Telepathica]:** You can project your thoughts into the minds of others across vast distances, enabling silent squad communication.

**Ork Boy**

*   **[Choppa/Slugga]:** You generate +1 Success on all melee attacks when charging directly at an enemy.
*   **[Sporeborn]:** If you are killed, you release spores that will eventually grow into new Orks, ensuring your legacy continues. (Narrative only).

**Eldar Ranger**

*   **[Wraith-Slip]:** You can move through dense foliage, ruins, or hazardous terrain at full speed without leaving a physical trace.
*   **[Path of the Outcast]:** You easily blend in with alien societies, underworld hubs, and mercenary bands, avoiding immediate hostile reactions.

**Rogue Trader**

*   **[Warrant of Trade]:** You possess a sacred writ allowing you to legally trade with xenos, mutants, and heretics without Imperial repercussion.
*   **[Orbital Asset]:** Once per session, you can call in a macro-cannon strike or drop-pod reinforcement from your orbiting voidship.
