# 01 - Identity & Origins

> "We are all refugees of a war that ended before we were born. You don’t get to choose where the rifts drop you, but you do get to choose what you become when you land."
> — Wayfarer's Journal, Entry 14

Identity is built upon three foundational pillars: your Origin, your Race, and your Background.

## The Origin Tag (Physiology & Material)
Your Origin dictates your physiological composition, the [Domain: Material] domain you occupy, your baseline Power Scale, and your inherent physiological traits. A character's `[Origin: X]` is inherently determined by their chosen `[Race: X]` (e.g., Selecting Elf automatically bestows the Biotic origin).

> **Origin Rules:** Origin is immutable and can only be changed by GM narrative. It automatically bestows its base features for free. Players cannot add tags to their Origin, and Origin tags cannot have Mastery advancements.

*   **[Origin: Biotic]:** An organic entity. Possesses the [Trait: Rapid Healing] tag (Minor Trauma stages heal in half the time).
*   **[Origin: Synthetic]:** A mechanical entity. Possesses [Trait: Defense Shielded 1] and is immune to biological toxins. Requires [Path Feature: Engineering] or [Path Feature: Technician] rather than standard medical tools for triage and stabilization.
*   **[Origin: Ascendant]:** A cosmic or relic entity. Possesses [Trait: Attuned]. Begins with +1 maximum [Capacity: Spells] capacity, but suffers [Trait: Defense Vulnerable] to [Domain: Disruptive].
*   **[Origin: Dimensional-Drifter]:** A multidimensional entity. Possesses [Status: Unstable], allowing you to re-roll a [System: Volatile] result once per session.


## The Race Tag
While your Origin defines your raw physiological or cosmic makeup (such as being organic or mechanical), your **Race Tag** defines the specific species or model you belong to. 

> **Race Rules:** Race is immutable. It determines the specific pool of Race Tags a player can choose from. Players choose exactly 1 Race Tag for free. Additional Race Tags may be purchased with EXP later, and Race Tags can have their Mastery advanced.

## Background Tags & Social Leverage
Backgrounds provide "Narrative Permission" to bypass social obstacles. Backgrounds do not grant dice; they grant automatic successes or information via the "Rule of Narrative Authority."

### Background Tags
* **[Background: Highborn]:** Possesses [Path Feature: Status]. Automatically gain audiences with rulers or executives.
* **[Path Feature: Criminal]:** Possesses [Path Feature: Underworld]. Automatically locate Black Markets and procure illegal goods.
* **[Path Feature: Scholar]:** Possesses [Path Feature: Well-Read]. Automatically identify [System: Power Scale] and device origins.
* **[Path Feature: Mercenary]:** Possesses [Status: Known]. Automatically gain [Path Feature: Professional] status with military/security forces.
* **[Path Feature: Commoner]:** Possesses [Status: Hidden]. Automatically pass through checkpoints and crowds without detection.

### The Rule of Narrative Authority
If a player’s Background provides the appropriate [System: Tag], they do not roll dice to achieve a social goal that aligns with their station.
*   **Example:** A [Background: Highborn] showing a signet ring to a guard is an automatic success.
*   **Exception:** If a player uses a Background tag for a goal outside its scope (e.g., a [Path Feature: Scholar] trying to intimidate a thug), they must roll their [Approach: Resonance] Approach as a standard check with no benefit.

> **GM's Veto:** If an application of a Background's Rule of Narrative Authority trivializes the central challenge of a mission, the GM may override the automatic success and instead require a Resonance or Cognition roll at [System: Disadvantage 2].
