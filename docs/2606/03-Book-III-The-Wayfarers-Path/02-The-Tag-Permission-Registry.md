# 02 - The Tag-Permission Registry

> "Specialization is a luxury for stable realities. Out here, you need a hammer, a scalpel, and a loaded gun. But everyone starts somewhere."
> — Intercepted Transmission, Wayfarer Designation: 'Patchwork'

The Tag-Permission Registry defines the core of a character's mechanical capabilities through the **1+1+1+2+1 Tag Structure**.

## The 1+1+1+2+1 Tag Structure (Classes & Paths)

During character creation, players define their capabilities by selecting their tags according to the 1+1+1+2+1 structure:

1. **Origin (1):** Bestows an innate biological or cosmic baseline ability (e.g., `[Origin: Biotic]`, `[Origin: Synthetic]`).
2. **Race (1):** Bestows 1 tag chosen from the specific features of your species (e.g., `[Race: Elf]`, `[Race: Android]`).
3. **Class (1):** Bestows 1 tag representing your primary profession or archetype (e.g., `[Class: Fighter]`, `[Class: Hacker]`). Players select exactly 1 Class Tag for free from their chosen Class pool.
4. **Path (2):** Bestows 2 tags chosen from the universal Path Registry to customize your build (e.g., `[Path Feature: Sniper]`, `[Path Feature: Technician]`). Players select exactly 2 Path Tags for free during character creation. Path tags can have their Mastery advanced.
    * **The Path Overflow:** All unchosen Class Tags from the primary class move into the universal Path Tag pool. A character's "Path" is an economic concept. When advancing, players spend EXP to buy tags from their available Path Pool. However, the tags retain their original prefixes (e.g., `[Class Feature]`, `[Racial Feature]`) denoting their literal multiversal origin.
5. **Background (1):** A character has exactly 1 Background, ever. They choose 1 Background Tag for free, which bestows its associated narrative features/traits (e.g., `[Background: Criminal]`, `[Background: Academic]`).
    * **The Alternating Ladder:** Background Tags can have their Mastery advanced. Doing so alternates the upgrades between its bestowed features (e.g., Tier 1 upgrades Feature A to Tier 1. Tier 2 upgrades Trait B to Tier 1. At Tier 4, both sub-features rest at Tier 2).

> **IMPORTANT - The Absolute Baseline:** The 1+1+1+2+1 structure should be considered the **Absolute Baseline** for all new character creations, converted characters, or characters advancing. This is **not** the max limit of tags that a character can possess for most categories. Any character that is not newly created may purchase additional Race, Class, or Path tags, provided they have the EXP to purchase them. However, you cannot purchase additional Origin or Background tags.

## The Tag-Permission Rule (Skill Resolution)
To maintain a math-less system, we use the Tag-Permission Rule for all non-combat actions. There are no numerical skill proficiencies.
* **No Tag:** If a player attempts a specialized task without the required Tag (e.g., hacking without [Status: Networked], or performing surgery without [Path Feature: Medical]), the action is attempted at [System: Disadvantage 2] and the action is considered [System: Volatile].
* **Have Tag:** If the player possesses the required Tag-Permission, they perform the action normally as a Standard Check.
* **Mastery Tag (EXP Upgrade):** If a player spends 8 EXP to upgrade a Tag to its [Path Feature: Mastery] form (e.g., [Class: Master Hacker], [Class: Master Surgeon]), they perform the action with [System: Advantage 1].

## The Path Book (Registry)

This dictionary-style list details the core Path Tags available during character creation and the mechanical permissions they grant.

*   **[Path Feature: Veteran]:**  
    *   *Permission:* The character may hold 2 AP from their turn to be used as Reactions instead of the standard 1 AP limit.
*   **[Path Feature: Technician]:** Possesses [Status: Networked] and [Origin: Mechanical]. 
    *   *Permission:* Expertise in electronics, software, and digital systems. You possess the [Status: Networked] condition when utilizing your gear. Hacking and electronic repair checks cost 1 to 3 AP depending on complexity. You possess the requisite knowledge to triage and stabilize [Origin: Synthetic] entities and characters with [Path Feature: Bionic] augmentations. Nullifies up to [System: Disadvantage 2] when interacting with foreign [Domain: Digital] infrastructure.
*   **[Path Feature: Survivalist]:**  
    *   *Permission:* The character is immune to [System: Disadvantage] penalties resulting from environmental hazards, extreme weather, or difficult natural terrain.
*   **[Path Feature: Conduit]:** Grants [Domain: Metaphysical]. 
    *   *Permission:* Grants the foundational Permission to safely channel raw magical Domains without automatically triggering [System: Volatile] feedback.
*   **[Path Feature: Infiltrator]:** Grants [Status: Shadowed]. 
    *   *Permission:* Gains [System: Advantage 1] on Vector rolls when operating in low light, dense cover, or when attempting stealth against unaware targets.
*   **[Background: Socialite]:** Grants [Path Feature: Influential]. 
    *   *Permission:* Once per session, the character may substitute their Resonance Approach for any other Approach roll by successfully leveraging social capital, calling in a favor, or manipulating the scenario.

*(Note: The Path Registry is fully expandable. Players may work with the GM to define new Path Tags for highly specialized concepts, provided they adhere to the single-permission structure).*
