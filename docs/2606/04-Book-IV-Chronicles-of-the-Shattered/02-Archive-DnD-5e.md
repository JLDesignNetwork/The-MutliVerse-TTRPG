### Origin & Innate Tag Permissions
*   **[Origin: Biotic]:** You are a living, biological organism. You require food, water, and oxygen, and are susceptible to organic toxins and diseases.
*   **[Trait: Darkvision]:** You ignore [System: Disadvantage] penalties for acting in complete or near-total darkness.
*   **[Path Feature: Elemental Breath]:** You possess a biological weapon (e.g., fire, acid) that you can use at short range without a weapon.
*   **[Trait: Defense Resistant X]:** You automatically ignore the first stage of trauma applied from your specific element or hazard type.
*   **[Trait: Attuned]:** You can passively sense the presence of active magic, ethereal entities, or rifts within your immediate vicinity.
*   **[Path Feature: Lucky]:** Once per session, you may force the GM to reroll a single die or anomaly result that directly affects you.
*   **[Path Feature: Relentless]:** The first time you would be [Status: Clinical Death] or drop to Trauma Stage 9, you instead remain conscious at Stage 8.
*   **[Path Feature: Intimidating]:** You gain [System: Advantage] on Resonance checks made to frighten, coerce, or bully an NPC.
*   **[Gear: Versatile]:** During character creation, you may select one additional Path Tag or one minor narrative permission.
*   **[Status: Shadowed]:** You gain [System: Advantage 1] on Vector rolls when attempting stealth in low light or dense cover.
*   **[Path Feature: Influential]:** Once per session, you may substitute your Resonance Approach for any other Approach roll by leveraging social capital.

> **Note on Path Tags:** The Recommended Path Tags listed below (such as [Path Feature: Veteran] or [Path Feature: Conduit]) are core capabilities. Their full mechanical permissions are detailed in **Book III: The Tag-Permission Registry**.

### Class Tag Permissions
When you select your archetype's Class Tag, it grants a permanent narrative and mechanical permission.

**Barbarian**
*   **[Path Feature: Rage]:** You can enter a state of fury, granting [Trait: Defense Resistant Kinetic] and [System: Advantage] on all melee Force checks, but preventing complex logical actions.
*   **[Path Feature: Juggernaut]:** You ignore movement penalties from heavy armor or carrying massive loads, and can smash through structural barriers without rolling.
*   **[Path Feature: Blood-Trance]:** You ignore all Status Effects from the Trauma Track until combat ends or you drop to Stage 9.

**Bard**
*   **[Path Feature: Inspire]:** You can spend an action to grant [System: Advantage] to an ally's next roll.
*   **[Path Feature: Lore-Keeper]:** You automatically know a relevant rumor or historical fact about any named location, faction, or artifact.
*   **[Path Feature: Skald]:** You can use Resonance instead of Force to intimidate or rally troops in mass combat.

**Cleric**
*   **[Path Feature: Divine Channel]:** You can spend Slots to perform miracles aligned with your deity's domain (e.g., healing, turning undead).
*   **[Class: War Priest]:** You can wield heavy weapons and heavy armor without the [System: Disadvantage] penalty usually applied to magic users.
*   **[Class: Life-Weaver]:** When using Triage to stabilize an ally, you restore two Trauma Stages instead of one.

**Druid**
*   **[Path Feature: Shapeshift]:** You can physically transform into beasts. While shifted, you use the physical tags of the beast instead of your own.
*   **[Path Feature: Nature's Wrath]:** You gain [System: Advantage] when manipulating environmental hazards (vines, weather, earth) to attack or trap foes.
*   **[Class: Spirit-Walker]:** You can see and communicate with spirits, elementals, and the lingering echoes of the dead.

**Fighter**
*   **[Path Feature: Mastery Weapon]:** Choose one weapon type. You always generate +1 Success on attacks with that weapon.
*   **[Path Feature: Tactician]:** You can assess a battlefield to identify the primary weakness or objective of the enemy forces automatically.
*   **[Path Feature: Champion]:** Your physical critical threshold is expanded; your Desperation Die explodes on a 9 or 10.

**Monk**
*   **[Path Feature: Ki Strike]:** Your unarmed strikes count as [Gear: Piercing] against standard kinetic armor.
*   **[Path Feature: Shadow-Step]:** You can instantly traverse short distances between areas of darkness without triggering movement penalties or overwatch.
*   **[Path Feature: Iron Body]:** You naturally possess [Trait: Defense Ablative 1] armor even when unarmored.

**Paladin**
*   **[Path Feature: Divine Smite]:** You can spend 1 Slot after a successful melee hit to add a massive burst of [Domain: Radiant] trauma.
*   **[Path Feature: Aura of Protection]:** Allies standing adjacent to you gain your highest Resistance tag.
*   **[Path Feature: Oathbound]:** You gain [System: Advantage] on any check that directly furthers your sworn oath, but suffer [System: Disadvantage] if you act against it.

**Ranger**
*   **[Path Feature: Favored Terrain]:** You cannot be tracked, and you ignore all natural difficult terrain penalties.
*   **[Path Feature: Beast-Master]:** You command a loyal animal companion that acts independently on your turn without costing your AP.
*   **[Path Feature: Hunter's Mark]:** You can mark a target; you never lose track of them as long as they remain on the same plane of existence.

**Rogue**
*   **[Path Feature: Sneak Attack]:** If you attack a target who is distracted or unaware, your attack is automatically treated as if you rolled 1 additional Success.
*   **[Class: Assassin]:** You gain [System: Advantage] on checks made to craft poisons, set traps, or strike from complete stealth.
*   **[Origin: Phantom]:** You leave no physical footprints, thermal signature, or scent trail unless you choose to.

**Sorcerer**
*   **[Path Feature: Metamagic]:** You can alter the tags of your spells on the fly (e.g., changing a [Domain: Thermal] blast to [Domain: Sonic], or changing [Gear: Loud] to [Gear: Silent]).
*   **[Path Feature: Wild Magic]:** You generate an extra d10 for spellcasting, but any non-success result triggers an immediate roll on the Anomaly Table.
*   **[Racial Feature: Draconic Bloodline]:** You naturally possess [System: Power Scale 2] physical durability and a passive breath weapon.

**Warlock**
*   **[Path Feature: Eldritch Pact]:** You can petition your patron for a supernatural favor or knowledge once per session, though it always comes with a narrative cost.
*   **[Path Feature: Hexblade]:** You can summon a bound weapon from the void that uses Cognition instead of Force for combat checks.
*   **[Class: Mind-Thief]:** You can read the surface thoughts of any sentient creature you make physical contact with.

**Wizard**
*   **[Gear: Spellbook]:** You can permanently learn any spell or ritual you encounter by spending downtime to transcribe it.
*   **[Class: Abjurer]:** You can project a protective barrier over yourself or an ally that absorbs 1 Trauma Stage per encounter.
*   **[Class: Evoker]:** Your area-of-effect spells automatically exclude your allies from the blast radius.
