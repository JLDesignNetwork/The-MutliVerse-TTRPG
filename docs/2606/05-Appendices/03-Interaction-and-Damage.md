# Interaction & Damage

Damage and interaction permissions define how combat and environment hazards resolve against entity states.

## Domains of Damage

* [Domain: Kinetic]: Direct physical impact, ballistic rounds, blade edges, crushing weights, or gravitational drops.
* [Domain: Thermal]: Extreme temperatures, thermal lasers, plasma arcs, or super-cooled freezing effects.
* [Domain: Disruptive]: Electromagnetic pulses (EMPs), raw data injection, sonic vibrations, or intrusive psychic shocks.
* [Domain: Entropy]: Necrotic energy, environmental radiation, chemical corrosion, or rapid decay forces.

## Interaction Permission Tags

* [System: Advantage 1]: Abstracted modifier adding favorable or unfavorable dice to a specific functional approach pool.
* [Path Feature: Exploding]: High-chaos or volatile assets. Rolling maximum values on a die grants an immediate additional roll.
* [Status: Flatline]: Lethal armor-piercing or definitive strikes. Completely bypasses standard damage mitigation tags unless overridden by an equivalent scale defense tag.
* [Status: Networked]: Actively transmitting or receiving digital signals. This tag is a mandatory requirement for any Cyber-Hacker or software-based interface attempt.
* [Trait: Attuned]: Permanently bonded to a specific soul signature or unique biometric marker. Prevents unauthorized usage or remote manipulation until severed by Metaphysical or Digital purification.
* [System: Volatile]: Unstable raw energy or magic. Triggers an automatic roll on the Multiverse Anomaly Table upon any failure condition, rather than just critical failures.

## The Multiverse Anomaly Table

When a failure triggers a [System: Volatile] asset (such as failing an unstable magic or tech check), roll 1d10:

* **1-2: Catastrophic Rift** - Pulls the character into a random, hostile dimension or violently severs their connection to the universe.
* **3-5: Devastating Backlash** - The spell or hack violently explodes. The user and all entities within Close Range take a [Status: Flatline] hit of [Domain: Entropy] or [Domain: Disruptive] damage.
* **6-8: Localized Physics Glitch** - Gravity inverts, or time stutters, inflicting [System: Disadvantage 2] on all characters for the encounter.
* **9: Volatile Surge** - The intended action succeeds, but deals 1 Trauma Point to the caster/user.
* **10: Cosmic Boon** - A glitch in the matrix. The action succeeds perfectly, and the character temporarily gains [System: Scale Shift 1] for the remainder of the encounter.
