# Cross-Universe Physics

When realities collide, the fundamental physics governing technology and magic can fail, corrupt, or alter depending on the host dimension.

## Technological Disparity & The Grid

### The [Lore: Grid-Locked] Tag
Advanced technology brought into lower-tech worlds will function based on its original parameters, but it cannot be replenished locally. Ammunition depletion or power-cell exhaustion permanently grants the asset the [Status: Dead] tag until returned to an appropriate power scale reality or jerry-rigged with extreme effort.

### Societal Reaction & The [System: Anomaly] Tag
Wielding highly advanced technology (e.g., a plasma rifle) in a primitive world grants [System: Advantage 2] on Awe or Intimidation checks. However, it permanently marks the user with the [System: Anomaly] tag, making them a prime target for local authorities, warlords, or deities.

## Divinity, Magic, & Disconnection

### The [Status: Severed] Tag
Crossing into a reality entirely devoid of your specific magical weave, divine pantheon, or mystical source immediately severs your connection. This nullifies all abilities tied to the [Domain: Metaphysical] tag until a new link is established.

### Source Manifestation & Tag Translation
When a severed entity manages to reconnect to a local power source (e.g., a Paladin drawing power from a Sci-Fi Machine God instead of their home deity), the underlying math and dice pools remain identical. However, the physical manifestation and output tags change. For instance, what used to deal Radiant damage now deals [Domain: Disruptive] or EMP damage.
