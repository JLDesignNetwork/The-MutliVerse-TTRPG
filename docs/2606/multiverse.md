# The Multiverse TTRPG Framework v2606.1.0-bs

## Chapter 1: The Shattered Cosmos

<a id="the-shattering"></a>
### The Shattering

> "Do not look at the rifts as doorways. Look at them as wounds. The multiverse is bleeding out, and the Architects are trying to cauterize the veins with us trapped inside. Keep your head down, keep your slots charged, and whatever you do—don't let them catch you tampering with the timeline."
> — Intercepted Transmission, Wayfarer Designation: 'Vagabond'

The Multiverse was not always a fractured mirror. It was once a singular, unified reality.

Eons ago, an ancient civilization—operating at the absolute pinnacle of existence, a theoretical [System: Power Scale 4]—attempted to unify time itself. They sought to bind every probability into a single, perfect timeline. Instead, their Hubris tore reality apart.

This cataclysm is known as **The Shattering**. The singular universe violently detonated into infinite, colliding dimensions. The laws of physics, magic, and digital space were ripped from their foundational moorings and scrambled across realities.

Every piece of rift-tech, every dimensional drive, and every anomalous artifact is actually *archaeo-tech*—dangerous, reverse-engineered fragments from this fallen civilization. You and your fellow Wayfarers are not gods traversing the planes; you are scavengers playing with matches in a powder keg. Every jump between realities is a violent trespassing across a cosmic wound.

<a id="zero-point-station"></a>
### Zero-Point Station (The Hub)

> "The smell hits you first. Ozone, blood, and burnt circuitry. Then the noise—a million dialects of survival echoing off non-Euclidean walls. They call it a station. I call it a life raft."
> — Wayfarer's Journal, Entry 41

In the wake of The Shattering, reality fractured. But at the dead center of the explosion, a pocket of null-space remained. Here lies **Zero-Point Station**, commonly known simply as **The Hub**.

The Hub is a massive, sprawling, non-Euclidean construct residing entirely outside of individual dimensions. It is the ultimate neutral zone where the boundaries of the Multiverse violently, yet peacefully, coexist. Here, the [Domain: Material], [Domain: Digital], and [Domain: Metaphysical] domains overlap seamlessly.

It is a desperate, bustling trade port and the last refuge of the displaced. In the neon-lit bazaars of the lower wards, a cyberpunk hacker from a dystopian metropolis might barter stolen subroutines for a magical grimoire peddled by a medieval wizard. It is a place of infinite possibility and infinite danger, where the only universal currency is survival.

<a id="factions-and-the-rift-guard"></a>
### Factions and The Rift-Guard

> "They don't care if a million innocent souls are consumed by the Void. They only care that the math balances. If pruning your reality keeps the rest of the sector stable, you won't even see the firing squad that pulls the trigger."
> — Wayfarer's Journal, Entry 12

Existence is governed by a central, terrifying authority: **The Architects**.

A consortium of [System: Power Scale 4] entities, The Architects survived The Shattering and now view the Multiverse as a diseased organism in need of aggressive triage. They do not rule; they manage. They enforce the **Stability Protocols**, strict laws designed to prevent further dimensional collapse.

To The Architects, containment is the only priority. Morality is a mathematical error. If a dimension becomes too anomalous or threatens the integrity of the Hub, they will willingly "prune" it—erasing the entire timeline to protect the remaining Multiverse.

#### The Rift-Guard
The Architects' enforcers are the **Rift-Guard**. Drawn from a thousand realities, they are the dimensional police force. Heavy-handed and dogmatic, they patrol the Hub and priority dimensions, hunting down anomalies, enforcing quarantines, and neutralizing threats to dimensional stability.

#### Allegiance and The Wayfarers
You are a Wayfarer, displaced by the chaotic currents of the Multiverse. Your allegiance is more than a tag on your sheet; it is a philosophical stance on how to survive the end of everything:

* **The Authority (The Rift-Guard):** Do you side with the Architects? Will you enforce order, even if it means destroying free will and sacrificing entire worlds for the "greater good"?
* **The Fugitives (Syndicates & Free-Runners):** Do you rebel against the pruning? Do you scavenge archaeo-tech to protect your own slice of reality, constantly dodging the Rift-Guard and surviving in the shadows?
* **The Unaligned:** Do you walk the razor's edge, selling your skills to the highest bidder in the bustling markets of Zero-Point Station?

<a id="the-grand-purpose"></a>
### The Grand Purpose

> "We aren't heroes. We are scavengers with a very specific set of skills, trying to make sense of a jigsaw puzzle where half the pieces are on fire and the other half are actively trying to kill us."
> — Intercepted Transmission, Wayfarer Designation: 'Vagabond'

The Multiverse TTRPG is not a game about saving the world. It is a game about surviving the collision of infinite worlds.

#### The Engine of Play
As Wayfarers, you are driven by mystery, curiosity, and the raw instinct to survive. You are navigating a multiverse where the specific dilemmas, horrors, and politics of infinite realities are violently bleeding into one another.

The Grand Purpose of your journey is defined by three pillars:
1. **Scavenge the Ruins:** You are constantly hunting for archaeo-tech, resources, and lore. Every piece of powerful technology or magic is a fragment of the civilization that broke the world.
2. **Navigate the Collision:** You must negotiate, fight, and think your way out of situations where high-fantasy magic collides with cyberpunk corporate espionage. The rules of reality are constantly in flux, and only the adaptable survive.
3. **Evade the Threat:** Whether you are dodging the dogmatic enforcement of the Rift-Guard, fleeing a cosmic anomaly, or surviving a dimensional collapse, the Multiverse is actively pushing back against your existence.

## Chapter 2: The Laws of Reality

<a id="core-resolution"></a>
### Core Resolution & The Dice Pool

> "The difference between life and death in the Hub isn't caliber. It's leverage. You don't fight fair against a cyber-troll; you drop the building on him."
> — Wayfarer's Journal, Entry 09

The Multiverse TTRPG operates on a d10 dice pool system that prioritizes narrative flow over granular math.

#### The Dice Pool
When attempting a risky or opposed action, you will roll a pool of d10s (10-sided dice) based on your Approaches or the Threat Rating of an adversary.

* **Success (7, 8, 9):** A die showing a 7, 8, or 9 is considered a standard success.
* **Critical Success (10):** A 10 is a critical success. It counts as a success **and explodes**, allowing you to immediately roll an additional d10 to add to your pool.
* **Critical Failure (1):** A 1 on a die is a critical failure.

#### Resolving Actions
The system avoids floating target numbers completely. Action resolution depends on whether you are acting against a static environment or an active opponent:

* **Unopposed Actions (The Static Environment):** Baseline requirement is always a **single success (rolling at least one 7+)**. Difficulty is exclusively scaled *before the roll* by applying System Disadvantage.
* **Passive Defense:** All combatants have a "Passive Defense" equal to 1 Success. If an attacker cannot generate at least 1 Success, the attack misses entirely.
* **Desperation Dodge:** If a player is completely out of AP and targeted by an attack, they may use a Desperation Dodge as a free action. The player rolls their [Approach: Vector] pool at [System: Disadvantage 2]. If they generate even 1 Success, they successfully negate the incoming hit.
* **Opposed Checks (The Success Clash):** When two entities act against each other, both roll their respective Approach pools. The winner is determined by **Total Successes**.
  * *Tie-Breaker:* Compare the highest single die face rolled among those successes (a 9 beats an 8).
  * *The Deadlock:* If successes and highest die faces are identical, the **Defender** wins the clash, maintaining the status quo.

#### The Zero-Die Mechanic
If circumstances or penalties reduce your dice pool to 0, you roll a single **Desperation Die**:
* The Desperation Die succeeds on a 9 or 10, and **explodes** on a 10. The resulting "Explosion Die" follows standard rules and succeeds on a 7+.
* Rolling a **1** results in a catastrophic failure.

#### Advantage and Disadvantage
* **[System: Disadvantage 1]:** Minor Hindrance. Lose 1 Die.
* **[System: Disadvantage 2]:** Severe Hindrance. Lose 2 Dice.
* **[System: Disadvantage 3]:** Critical Hindrance. Lose 3 Dice (drops to Zero-Die Mechanic if pool reaches 0).
* **[System: Advantage 1]:** Tactical Edge. Add +1 Die.
* **[System: Advantage 2]:** Severe Dominance. Add +2 Dice. Total applied Advantage to a single dice pool cannot exceed +2.

#### The Overcap Momentum System
If a player orchestrates a scenario generating a 3rd or 4th Advantage, those excess points convert into **Momentum Tokens** in a shared crew pool:
* Any player may spend 1 Momentum Token on their turn to grant themselves [System: Advantage 1] or ignore 1 level of Recoil.
* **All unspent Momentum Tokens evaporate the moment the combat encounter ends.**

<a id="the-action-economy"></a>
### The Action Economy & Turn Order

> "A bullet travels faster than a spell. A thought travels faster than both. Act first or don't act at all."
> — Intercepted Transmission, Wayfarer Designation: 'Vagabond'

#### Initiative & The Handoff
Combat begins with the defining action. If in neutral standoff:
1. **Scale Dominance:** The entity with the highest Power Scale acts first.
2. **Sensory & Spatial Permissions:** Environmental tags dictate advantage (e.g. [Trait: Darkvision] in pitch black).
3. **The Vector Clash:** Compare base Vector approaches.

**Popcorn Initiative:** Once a character finishes their turn, they choose who acts next—an ally or an enemy—among those who haven't acted yet this round. The last character to act chooses who begins the next round.
* Spend a Momentum Token to interrupt and take the turn immediately.
* Hold up to 1 AP from your turn to use as a Reaction during the following round.

#### Action Points (AP)
Every entity operates on a baseline of **3 Action Points (AP)** per turn (maximum cap of 5 AP):
* **1 AP:** Move, standard attack, swap weapon, or basic maneuver.
* **2 AP:** Channel a stable spell, Buddy-Aid, or fire an unbraced heavy weapon.
* **3 AP:** Critical Triage, cast a massive spell, or execute an extreme sprint.

#### The Recoil Rule (Multi-Attacks)
* The first attack roll of a turn is normal.
* The second attack roll suffers [System: Disadvantage 1].
* The third attack roll suffers [System: Disadvantage 2].

#### Volatile Synchronization (The "Called Combo")
Players can execute combined maneuvers to bridge Power Scale gaps or unleash devastating elemental combinations:
1. **The Declaration:** Player A spends AP to prep the combo and designates Player B.
2. **The Execution:** When Player B's turn arrives, they spend AP to complete the Sync. Both players roll simultaneously.
3. **The Risk:** The entire combined maneuver automatically gains the [System: Volatile] tag. If *either* player fails to generate at least 1 success, the combo collapses catastrophically, spent AP is lost, and the **Threat Track immediately escalates by +1**.

<a id="the-status-manifest"></a>
### The Status Manifest & Body Map

> "A stim-pack might keep your heart beating, but it won’t reattach your arm. Know your limits before someone else tests them for you."
> — Intercepted Transmission, Wayfarer Designation: 'Bones'

Health, damage, and stability are tracked through state-of-being tracks rather than numerical hit points.

#### The 9-Stage Trauma Track (Biotic / Non-Synthetic)
* **Minor Trauma ([Status: Trauma Stage 1]–[Status: Trauma Stage 3]):** Flesh wounds and superficial damage. No immediate mechanical penalty.
* **Severe Trauma ([Status: Trauma Stage 4]–[Status: Trauma Stage 6]):** Deep structural damage. Imposes specific localized limb penalties on the Body Map.
* **Critical Trauma ([Status: Trauma Stage 7]–[Status: Trauma Stage 9]):** Lethal or incapacitating damage.
* **The Death Timer:** Reaching [Status: Trauma Stage 9] triggers [Status: Clinical Death]. The character gains an active death timer equal to their **Endurance Approach rating (in rounds)**. Any ally can spend 3 AP and utilize [Path Feature: Medical] or magical healing to resuscitate the character.

#### The 9-Stage Stability Track (Synthetic)
* Synthetic entities ([Origin: Synthetic] or full cybernetics) track stress, electrical disruption, and software corruption from Stage 1 to Stage 9. Targeted by [Domain: Digital] and [Domain: Disruptive] damage.
* Reaching Stage 9 inflicts immediate [Status: System Shutdown] (paralyzed/offline) until rebooted via 3 AP and [Path Feature: Technician] or [Path Feature: Engineering].

<a id="the-body-map"></a>
#### The Body Map & Localized Trauma
When suffering Severe (Stages 4–6) or Critical Trauma (Stages 7–8), damage is assigned via the 3-Step Hierarchy:
1. **Narrative Circumstance:** Scenario dictates hit location (e.g. Landmine hits Legs).
2. **The Called Shot:** Attacker declared target location *before* rolling.
3. **Defender's Choice:** Defender chooses location fitting the narrative.

**Qualitative Limb Penalties:**
* **[Status: Maimed] (Arms):** Suffer [System: Disadvantage 2] on manual tasks; cannot use two-handed items.
* **[Status: Hobbled] (Legs):** Cannot sprint; moving beyond Close Range requires two full turns of movement.
* **[Status: Disoriented] (Head):** Lose environmental awareness; cannot use Cognition approach; lose sensory advantages.
* **[Status: Staggered] (Torso):** Cannot hold AP for Reactions or utilize [Status: Overclocked] abilities.

#### Triage & Recovery
* **Mid-Combat Triage:** Mundane triage can only be performed **once per combat encounter**:
  * *Quick Patch (1 AP):* Suppresses 1 minor trauma stage.
  * *Field Surgery (2 AP):* Heals 2 trauma stages (Requires [Path Feature: Medical] or [Path Feature: Technician]).
  * *Critical Triage (3 AP):* Heals 3 trauma stages and stabilizes dying entity.
* **Magical Healing:** Bypasses the "once per combat" limit; governed by specific spell descriptions.
* **Recovery Timelines:** Minor (24–72h; halved for Biotics), Severe (3–6 weeks in Sterile environment), Critical (requires surgical reconstruction or Metaphysical miracles).

<a id="the-threat-system"></a>
### The Threat System & Reality Pushback

> "You think you can just bend reality until it breaks and nobody will notice? The Hub notices everything. And it always corrects the ledger."
> — Intercepted Transmission, Wayfarer Designation: 'Cipher'

The Threat Track is a shared GM pool (1 to 10) tracking dimensional instability and Architect response:

* **Tier 1: Systemic Friction (Track 1–3):** Comms static, minor sensory glitches, automated drone sweeps.
* **Tier 2: The Pushback (Track 4–6):** Localized anomalies (gravity inversion, time loop), Bounty posted, Rift-Guard squad deployed.
* **Tier 3: Critical Instability (Track 7–9):** Hostile dimensional rifts, "Execute on Sight" warrant, shifting facility geometry.
* **Tier 4: The Erasure Event (Track 10):** Architect Prime manifests or Zero-Point Erasure deployed. 3 rounds to evacuate before total sector deletion.

**Cooling the Track:** Fleeing sector (reduces by half), Laying Low in safehouse (-2 stages), Bribes/Data Wipes.

<a id="the-scale-of-reality"></a>
### The Scale of Reality & Power Scales

* **[System: Power Scale 1 Primal & Archaic]:** Low-tech, medieval fantasy, mundane humans, traditional swords, flintlocks.
* **[System: Power Scale 2 Augmented & Industrial]:** Modern firearms, steampunk mechanisms, early cybernetics, intermediate supernatural potencies.
* **[System: Power Scale 3 Advanced & Stellar]:** High sci-fi, starships, powered mechs, heavy power armor, elder supernatural entities, Mega-Damage Capacity (M.D.C.).
* **[System: Power Scale 4 Cosmic & Singular]:** Reality-warping deities, outer dimensional godheads, absolute planetary threats.

#### Scale Dominance & Friction
Lower-scale entities attacking higher-scale entities face **Friction** (automatic failure or severe disadvantage).
* **Hard-Cap:** An attack cannot inflict damage on an entity 2+ Power Scales higher without specific [Gear: Piercing] or [Status: Flatline] tags matching target composition.
* **[System: Scale Shift +/- X]:** Mastery action burning 1 Slot and 2 AP to temporarily act as 1 Power Scale higher for one encounter.

## Chapter 3: The Wayfarer's Path

<a id="identity-and-origins"></a>
### Identity, Origins, & Backgrounds

> "We are all refugees of a war that ended before we were born. You don’t get to choose where the rifts drop you, but you do get to choose what you become when you land."
> — Wayfarer's Journal, Entry 14

#### The Origin Tag (Physiology & Material)
Origin is immutable and automatically bestows its base features:
* **[Origin: Biotic]:** Organic entity. Possesses [Trait: Rapid Healing] (Minor Trauma heals in half time).
* **[Origin: Synthetic]:** Mechanical entity. Possesses [Trait: Defense Shielded 1]; immune to biological toxins; repaired via Engineering/Technician.
* **[Origin: Ascendant]:** Cosmic/relic entity. Possesses [Trait: Attuned] (+1 Spell capacity, Vulnerable to Disruptive).
* **[Origin: Dimensional-Drifter]:** Multidimensional entity. Possesses [Status: Unstable] (re-roll a [System: Volatile] roll once per session).

#### Background Tags & The Rule of Narrative Authority
Backgrounds provide narrative permission to bypass social obstacles without rolling dice:
* **[Background: Highborn]:** Possesses [Path Feature: Status] (Automatic audiences with rulers).
* **[Path Feature: Criminal]:** Possesses [Path Feature: Underworld] (Locate black markets effortlessly).
* **[Path Feature: Scholar]:** Possesses [Path Feature: Well-Read] (Identify Power Scale and device origins).
* **[Path Feature: Mercenary]:** Possesses [Status: Known] (Professional standing with military forces).
* **[Path Feature: Commoner]:** Possesses [Status: Hidden] (Pass checkpoints and crowds unnoticed).

<a id="the-tag-permission-registry"></a>
### The Tag-Permission Registry & 1+1+1+2+1 Baseline

> "Specialization is a luxury for stable realities. Out here, you need a hammer, a scalpel, and a loaded gun. But everyone starts somewhere."
> — Intercepted Transmission, Wayfarer Designation: 'Patchwork'

#### The 1+1+1+2+1 Tag Structure
Every starting character selects tags according to this absolute baseline:
1. **Origin (1):** Innate biological/cosmic baseline (`[Origin: Biotic]`, `[Origin: Synthetic]`).
2. **Race (1):** Species/model feature (`[Race: Elf]`, `[Race: Android]`).
3. **Class (1):** Primary profession archetype (`[Class: Fighter]`, `[Class: Hacker]`). Unchosen class tags move into Path pool.
4. **Path (2):** Customization build tags (`[Path Feature: Sniper]`, `[Path Feature: Technician]`). Can advance Mastery.
5. **Background (1):** Social identity (`[Background: Criminal]`, `[Background: Academic]`).

#### The Tag-Permission Rule (Skill Resolution)
* **No Tag:** Specialized tasks without the required Tag are rolled at [System: Disadvantage 2] and are [System: Volatile].
* **Have Tag:** Standard check with no penalty.
* **Mastery Tag (EXP Upgrade):** Performed with [System: Advantage 1].

<a id="approaches-and-arrays"></a>
### Approaches & The 12-Die Array

> "Don't tell me what you learned in the academy. Tell me how you hit when you're cornered, how you jump when the floor gives out, and how you think when the air gets thin. That's what matters."
> — Wayfarer's Journal, Entry 22

Characters distribute exactly **12 Dice** across 5 Approaches:
* **Force:** Raw physical power, lifting, striking, structural pressure.
* **Vector:** Agility, speed, precision, coordination, reflexes.
* **Endurance:** Stamina, physical resilience, biological resistance.
* **Cognition:** Logic, intellect, memory, technical interface, analysis.
* **Resonance:** Charisma, intimidation, empathy, willpower, metaphysical attunement.

*Cap:* Maximum 4 Dice in any single Approach at character creation (expandable to 5 with EXP). Floor: 0 Dice (uses Desperation Die).

Equipment profiles consist of 4 elements: Approach used, Optimal Range band, Damage Domain, and Weapon Tags.

3. **Cantrip Slots (Minor Memory):** Equal to Mental Approach Rating (1 to 5 slots).

## Chapter 4: Adversaries & Threat Profiles

<a id="running-adversaries"></a>
### Running Adversaries & Hierarchy

1. **Minions (The Swarm):** Threat 2 Dice, 1 Major Wound to kill, 1 AP/turn.
2. **Veterans (The Tactical Baseline):** Threat 3 Dice, 2 hits (1 to strip Ablative defense, 1 to kill), 2 AP/turn.
3. **Elites (The Tactical Threat):** Specific stat approaches, 2 full Major Wounds to kill, 3 AP/turn, 1+ signature Tags.
4. **Bosses (The Mirror Match):** Full Character Sheet (5 Approaches), full 9-Stage Trauma Track, 3–5 AP/turn.

#### Morale & Motivational Anchors
* **[Path Feature: Morale 1] (Self-Preservation):** Breaks at first sign of defeat.
* **[Path Feature: Morale 2] (Professional):** Holds lines until strategically unviable.
* **[Path Feature: Morale 3] (Zealot):** Fights through grievous injury.
* **[Path Feature: Mindless]:** Immune to morale failure.
* *Break Behaviors:* Panicked Rout (sprint away), Desperate Rescue (drag wounded allies), Tactical Fallback (covering fire retreat).
