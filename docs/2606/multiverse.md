## 1. System Overview & Core Philosophy

The Multiverse TTRPG is a **math-less, tag-based d10 dice pool engine** engineered specifically for high-velocity narrative gameplay and seamless cross-genre compatibility. Whether running gritty cyberpunk, high fantasy, grimdark space-opera, or mashing them all together inside volatile dimensional rifts, the framework unifies conflicting physics, magic, and technology through a standardized **Tag-Permission** architecture.

```
       ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
       │             THE UNIFIED RESOLUTION ENGINE              │
       │                                                        │
       │   • 7+ on a d10 is a Success (Exploding 10s)           │
       │   • Front-End Difficulty (Disadvantage strips dice)     │
       │   • 1+1+1+2+1 Tag-Permission Character Baseline        │
       │   • 9-Stage Trauma (Biotic) vs Stability (Synthetic)   │
       │   • Vertical Power Scales (Scale 1 Street to 4 Cosmic) │
       └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Core Mechanical Pillars
1. **Math-Less Resolution:** Success is determined by counting dice showing **7 or higher**. Target numbers do not float; difficulty is represented physically on the front end by stripping dice from your pool ([System: Disadvantage]) or granting tactical dice ([System: Advantage]).
2. **The Unified Tag System:** Capabilities, gear, racial traits, and magic are codified through semantic tags (`[Origin:]`, `[Trait:]`, `[Gear:]`, `[Domain:]`, `[Scale:]`), granting explicit mechanical and narrative permissions.
3. **Multi-Genre Compatibility:** External tabletop roleplaying systems (D&D 5e, Cyberpunk RED, Warhammer 40k, Call of Cthulhu, Shadowrun, etc.) translate directly into the framework via **The Infinite Archive** without requiring mathematical conversion formulas.
4. **Scale Dominance & Friction:** Vertical power disparities (from street-level mercenaries to planetary godheads) are governed by Power Scales (1 to 4), dictating automated friction and threshold damage.
5. **Dual-Track Harm:** Replaces arbitrary hit point arithmetic with qualitative **9-Stage Trauma Tracks** (for biological entities) and **9-Stage Stability Tracks** (for synthetic/digital constructs).

## 3. Quick Reference: The 5-Step Resolution Loop

When any risky or contested action occurs at the table:

1. **Pick the Approach:** Identify the applicable Approach ([Approach: Force], [Approach: Vector], [Approach: Cognition], [Approach: Insight], or [Approach: Resonance]).
2. **Apply Tag Permissions:** Check relevant `[Origin:]`, `[Trait:]`, `[Gear:]`, and `[Path:]` tags to establish tactical permissions.
3. **Determine Advantage/Disadvantage:** Stripping dice for hindrances ([System: Disadvantage 1-3]) or adding bonus dice ([System: Advantage 1-2]). Convert excess Advantage to [System: Momentum Tokens].
4. **Roll the Pool (d10s):**
   * **7, 8, 9:** Standard Success.
   * **10:** Critical Success + **Explodes** (roll an additional d10).
   * **1:** Critical Failure.
5. **Resolve the Outcome:**
   * **Unopposed:** 1 Success (7+) achieves the goal.
   * **Opposed:** Most successes wins. Ties compare highest single die face; deadlocks favor the Defender.
