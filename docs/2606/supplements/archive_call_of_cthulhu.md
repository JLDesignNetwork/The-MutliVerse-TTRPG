# The Infinite Archive: Call of Cthulhu v2606.1.0-bs

This archive provides conversion baselines for the investigators of Call of Cthulhu. CoC operates on a very low Power Scale, emphasizing survival and psychological trauma over combat prowess.

---

## Core Origin
All investigators possess the following baseline:
* **Origin Tag:** [Origin: Biotic] (Human)
* **Innate Traits:** [Trait: Defense Vulnerable Metaphysical] (Extremely susceptible to cosmic horror).

---

## Sanity and Stability
In CoC conversions, the Threat System and Anomaly Table should heavily focus on the [Domain: Metaphysical] domain, applying permanent psychological Status Tags (e.g., [Path Feature: Phobia], [Path Feature: Paranoia]) and Stability Track stress rather than physical Trauma.
