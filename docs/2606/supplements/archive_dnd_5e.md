### Origin & Innate Tag Permissions
* **[Origin: Biotic]:** Biological organism requiring food, water, and oxygen; susceptible to toxins.
* **[Trait: Darkvision]:** Ignore [System: Disadvantage] penalties for acting in complete or near-total darkness.
* **[Path Feature: Elemental Breath]:** Biological weapon (fire, acid, lightning) usable at short range without gear.
* **[Trait: Defense Resistant X]:** Automatically ignore the first stage of trauma applied from specified element.
* **[Trait: Attuned]:** Passively sense active magic, ethereal entities, or rifts nearby.
* **[Path Feature: Lucky]:** Once per session, force a reroll on a single die or anomaly result affecting you.
* **[Path Feature: Relentless]:** Drop to Stage 8 instead of Stage 9 the first time you would suffer Clinical Death.
* **[Path Feature: Intimidating]:** [System: Advantage] on Resonance checks to frighten or coerce NPCs.
* **[Gear: Versatile]:** Select 1 additional Path Tag or minor narrative permission during character creation.
* **[Status: Shadowed]:** [System: Advantage 1] on Vector rolls when attempting stealth in low light or dense cover.
* **[Path Feature: Influential]:** Once per session, substitute Resonance for any other Approach roll by leveraging social capital.

### Class Tag Permissions

**Barbarian**
* **[Path Feature: Rage]:** Enter a state of fury, granting [Trait: Defense Resistant Kinetic] and [System: Advantage] on melee Force checks.
* **[Path Feature: Juggernaut]:** Ignore movement penalties from heavy armor/loads; smash structural barriers without rolling.
* **[Path Feature: Blood-Trance]:** Ignore all Status Effects from the Trauma Track until combat ends or you drop to Stage 9.

**Bard**
* **[Path Feature: Inspire]:** Spend an action to grant [System: Advantage] to an ally's next roll.
* **[Path Feature: Lore-Keeper]:** Automatically know a relevant rumor or historical fact about any named location, faction, or artifact.
* **[Path Feature: Skald]:** Use Resonance instead of Force to intimidate or rally troops in mass combat.

**Cleric**
* **[Path Feature: Divine Channel]:** Channel pure divine energy to immediately heal an ally for 2 Trauma Stages or turn undead/demonic entities.
* **[Class: War Priest]:** Use Resonance to guide your weapon strikes, gaining [System: Advantage] against heretics or fiends.
* **[Class: Life-Weaver]:** When using Mid-Combat Triage, you heal +1 additional Trauma Stage without expending extra AP.

**Druid**
* **[Path Feature: Shapeshift]:** Take the form of a beast, swapping your Approach pool and gaining physical traits (e.g., flight, aquatic breathing).
* **[Path Feature: Nature's Wrath]:** Call upon the environment to entangle, trip, or restrain enemies within Close Range.
* **[Class: Spirit-Walker]:** Speak with plants and animals, gaining automatic narrative insight without rolling.

**Fighter**
* **[Path Feature: Mastery Weapon]:** You cannot be disarmed, and your primary weapon attacks ignore standard cover.
* **[Path Feature: Tactician]:** Re-order the initiative flow, allowing an ally to act immediately after your turn.
* **[Path Feature: Champion]:** Your critical hits explode on both 9s and 10s.

**Monk**
* **[Path Feature: Ki Strike]:** Unarmed attacks deal [Domain: Kinetic] damage that bypasses [Trait: Defense Ablative] armor.
* **[Path Feature: Shadow-Step]:** Teleport between patches of shadow within Close Range as a free action.
* **[Path Feature: Iron Body]:** Spend 1 AP to gain [Trait: Defense Resistant Kinetic] until the start of your next turn.

**Paladin**
* **[Path Feature: Divine Smite]:** Add [Domain: Radiant] damage to a melee strike, dealing double successes against fiends or undead.
* **[Path Feature: Aura of Protection]:** Allies within Close Range gain +1 passive defense against magical hazards.
* **[Path Feature: Oathbound]:** Immune to magical fear and charm effects.

**Ranger**
* **[Path Feature: Favored Terrain]:** You and your crew ignore movement penalties in wilderness environments.
* **[Path Feature: Beast-Master]:** Command an animal companion that acts with 2 AP on your turn.
* **[Path Feature: Hunter's Mark]:** Designate a quarry to gain [System: Advantage] on all tracking and attack checks against that target.

**Rogue**
* **[Path Feature: Sneak Attack]:** Gain +2 Successes on melee or ranged attacks against unaware or flanked targets.
* **[Class: Assassin]:** On the first round of combat, your successful attacks deal lethal [Status: Flatline] damage.
* **[Origin: Phantom]:** Slip through solid walls or physical barriers once per encounter.

**Sorcerer**
* **[Path Feature: Metamagic]:** Modify spell parameters (range, area, domain) on the fly by spending 1 AP.
* **[Path Feature: Wild Magic]:** Roll on the Anomaly Table deliberately to trigger unpredictable reality warps.
* **[Racial Feature: Draconic Bloodline]:** Gain natural [Trait: Defense Ablative 1] scales and elemental resistance.

**Warlock**
* **[Path Feature: Eldritch Pact]:** Cast destructive Eldritch Blasts at Long Range without consuming spell slots.
* **[Path Feature: Hexblade]:** Use Resonance instead of Force/Vector for melee weapon attacks.
* **[Class: Mind-Thief]:** Read surface thoughts of a target during social or combat encounters.

**Wizard**
* **[Gear: Spellbook]:** Prepare and cast spells from any domain you have transcribed into your grimoire.
* **[Class: Abjurer]:** Create magical wards that absorb up to 3 stages of incoming Trauma for yourself or allies.
* **[Class: Evoker]:** Shape area-of-effect spells so they completely avoid hitting friendly targets.
