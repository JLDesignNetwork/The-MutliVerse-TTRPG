# Archive: D&D Conditions & Backgrounds

This archive contains The Multiverse mechanical conversions for D&D 5e / 2024 legacy features.

## Status

*   **[Status: Blinded]:** A creature with the [Blinded] status cannot perceive visually. It automatically fails any Test requiring visual input, such as [Approach: Precision] or [Approach: Insight] Tests. Attacks made against the [Blinded] creature gain [System: Advantage 1], and the [Blinded] creature's own attacks suffer [System: Disadvantage 1].
*   **[Status: Charmed]:** A creature with the [Charmed] tag cannot willingly take hostile actions or target the source of the [Charmed] effect with harmful abilities. The source of the [Charmed] effect gains [System: Advantage 1] on all [Approach: Presence] Tests when interacting socially with the charmed creature.
*   **[Status: Deafened]:** A creature with this status cannot hear. They automatically fail any Test [Approach: Insight] that explicitly relies on auditory input. They have [System: Disadvantage 1] on Tests [Approach: Insight] for detecting or perceiving through sound. They cannot understand spoken language.
*   **[Status: Debilitated]:** The [Debilitated] status tracks extreme fatigue across 6 cumulative stages. Each stage applies its effect and all lower stages. Stage 1: All non-combat Tests are made with [System: Disadvantage 1]. Stage 2: Movement Speed is halved. Stage 3: All offensive actions and resistance Tests are made with [System: Disadvantage 1]. Stage 4: Suffers 1 additional stage of Trauma from all sources. Stage 5: Movement Speed is reduced to 0. Stage 6: Character suffers [Status: Clinical Death] and at risk of permanent demise. A Long Rest with adequate sustenance reduces the [Debilitated] stage by 1. This status translates the legacy 'Exhaustion' condition, mapping its levels to cumulative stages of debilitation and its HP reduction to increased Trauma susceptibility.
*   **[Status: Frightened]:** A creature with the Frightened status cannot willingly move closer to the source of its fear. While the source of its fear is within its line of sight, the creature makes all [Approach: Force], [Approach: Precision], [Approach: Insight], and [Approach: Presence] Tests with [System: Disadvantage 1].
*   **[Status: Grappled]:** A creature that gains the [Grappled] tag due to a grapple has its movement completely halted. It cannot move or benefit from any effects that would increase its movement speed. This condition persists until the grappler suffers [Status: Clinical Death], or until an external force or effect moves the [Grappled] creature out of the grappler's immediate reach.
*   **[Status: Incapacitated]:** A creature with the [Incapacitated] tag cannot spend AP for actions or reactions.
*   **[Status: Invisible]:** A creature with the [Invisible] status cannot be directly targeted by senses unless the observer possesses a special sense or magical means of detection. Its location can still be inferred by sound or tracks. Attack Tests made against an [Invisible] creature suffer [System: Disadvantage 1]. Attack Tests made by an [Invisible] creature gain [System: Advantage 1].
*   **[Status: Paralyzed]:** A creature suffering from paralysis is [Restrained], cannot take actions, move, or speak. It automatically suffers [System: Disadvantage 1] on all [Approach: Force] and [Approach: Precision] Tests. Attackers gain [System: Advantage 1] on all attacks made against the creature. Additionally, any successful attack made against the creature from within 5ft is considered a critical hit.
*   **[Status: Petrified]:** The target is transformed into a solid, inanimate substance. It gains the [Restrained] tag, cannot speak, and is unaware of its surroundings. Attackers gain [System: Advantage 1] on Tests against the target. The target automatically fails all [Approach: Force] and [Approach: Precision] Tests. It gains resistance to all forms of Trauma and is immune to Biotic effects. Any existing Biotic effects are suspended. Its weight increases tenfold, and it ceases aging.
*   **[Status: Infected]:** While [Infected], you suffer [System: Disadvantage 1] on all Tests involving [Approach: Force], [Approach: Precision], [Approach: Fortitude], [Approach: Insight], and [Approach: Presence].
*   **[Status: Prone]:** A creature with the Prone status has its movement speed halved. It makes [Approach: Force] and [Approach: Precision] Tests for attacks with [System: Disadvantage 1]. Attackers within 5ft of a Prone creature make [Approach: Force] and [Approach: Precision] Tests for attacks with [System: Advantage 1]. Attackers beyond 5ft of a Prone creature make [Approach: Force] and [Approach: Precision] Tests for attacks with [System: Disadvantage 1]. A creature can remove the Prone status by spending 1 AP.
*   **[Status: Restrained]:** A creature with the [Restrained] tag cannot move. All [Approach: Force] and [Approach: Precision] Tests made by the creature suffer [System: Disadvantage 1]. All [Approach: Precision] Tests made by the creature to defend against incoming effects suffer [System: Disadvantage 1]. All [Approach: Force] and [Approach: Precision] Tests made to attack the creature gain [System: Advantage 1].
*   **[Status: Stunned]:** A Stunned creature suffers [Status: Stunned], unable to take any actions, 1 APs, or reactions. They cannot move and can only speak falteringly. The creature automatically suffers [System: Disadvantage 1] on all [Approach: Force] and [Approach: Precision] Tests. Additionally, any Tests made to affect the Stunned creature (such as an attack) gain [System: Advantage 1].
*   **[Status: Unconscious]:** A character suffering from the Unconscious state cannot take actions, reactions, or move. They are unaware of their surroundings and drop anything they are holding. While Unconscious, any Test requiring [Approach: Force] or [Approach: Precision] automatically fails. Attackers gain [System: Advantage 1] on all attacks made against an Unconscious character. Any attack that successfully hits an Unconscious character from within 5ft inflicts an additional 1 to 4 stages of Trauma of the attack's type.

## Background

*   **[Background: Acolyte]:** You have dedicated your life to a deity or spiritual tradition. This grants you deep knowledge of its tenets and rituals, providing [System: Advantage 1] on [Approach: Insight] Tests when recalling religious lore or understanding spiritual practices. Your devotion earns you respect within your faith's community, granting [System: Advantage 1] on [Approach: Presence] Tests when seeking aid or hospitality from fellow worshipers or religious institutions. When recovering within a consecrated space of your faith, you gain [System: Advantage 1] on [Approach: Fortitude] Tests to reduce Trauma stages.
*   **[Background: Criminal]:** This background provides narrative context for a character's past involvement in criminal activities. When attempting tasks related to thievery, stealth, or social manipulation within criminal circles, the character gains [System: Advantage 1] on [Approach: Precision] or [Approach: Presence] tests.
*   **[Background: Sage]:** You possess an innate ability to locate sources of information. When seeking specific lore or knowledge, you automatically discern where and from whom that information can be obtained, such as a specific library, scholar, or ancient text. This ability functions as a narrative bypass for information gathering, rather than a direct test.
*   **[Background: Soldier]:** Passive: You retain a military rank from your past service. This grants you inherent social influence and authority within military organizations. Soldiers of lower rank, especially those loyal to your former organization, will recognize your standing and defer to you. You can leverage this rank to requisition basic supplies, secure passage through military encampments and fortresses, and reliably find lodging within such facilities, provided you have no history of desertion or severe infractions. This also implies a potential for regular payment if still actively associated with a military.
