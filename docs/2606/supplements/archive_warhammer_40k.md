### Origin & Innate Tag Permissions
* **[Origin: Biotic]:** Living biological organism susceptible to organic toxins.
* **[Gear: Versatile]:** Select 1 additional Path Tag or minor narrative permission during creation.
* **[Trait: Defense Resistant X]:** Automatically ignore the first stage of trauma from designated element.
* **[Trait: Defense Ablative X]:** Naturally absorb the first X stages of physical trauma without health degradation.
* **[Path Feature: Acrobat]:** [System: Advantage] on Vector/Force checks in difficult terrain.
* **[Path Feature: Psionic]:** Sense minds and warp anomalies nearby.
* **[Path Feature: Relentless]:** Drop to Stage 8 instead of Stage 9 the first time you would suffer Clinical Death.
